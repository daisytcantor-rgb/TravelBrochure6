Luzon

Manila

1. Manila, Intramuros

The historic Walled City built in 1571. Old Spanish churches, Fort Santiago, and cobblestone streets show the colonial history of the Philippines.

Ifugao

2. Banaue, Rice Terraces

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, these 2,000-year-old terraces were carved by Ifugaos. Called the “Eighth Wonder of the World,” they look like giant green stairways in the mountains.

Visayas

Aklan

1. Boracay, Aklan

This famous island is known worldwide for powdery white sand, crystal-clear water, beautiful sunsets, and water activities.

Plan with questions
Bohol

2. Bohol - Chocolate Hills + Tarsier

Bohol is home to 1,268 Chocolate Hills that turn brown in the dry season, and the tiny tarsier, one of the smallest primates in the world. Discover nature and wildlife in one place.

See the landscapes

Mindanao

Surigao del Norte

1. Siargao, Surigao del Norte

The Surfing Capital of the Philippines is famous for Cloud 9 waves, coconut-lined roads, and turquoise lagoons such as Sugba Lagoon.

Davao

2. Davao City / Samal Island

Davao is home to Mt. Apo, the highest mountain in the Philippines. Just across from the city, Samal Island is known for white-sand beaches and clear waters only minutes away.

A useful habit

Leave space in the itinerary.

Travel days can change because of weather, ferry schedules, or a conversation that runs long. Our imaginary packing list includes water, sun protection, a reusable bag, and enough time to notice where we are.

Before you publish a guide

  • Check that the place name is spelled correctly.
  • Credit the photographer or use your own photo.
  • Write one practical tip and one personal observation.
  • Remind readers to respect local people and places.
