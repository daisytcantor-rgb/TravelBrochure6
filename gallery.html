LUZON

LUZON

Manila, Intramuros

Halo-halo

The historic Walled City of Manila, filled with cobblestone streets, heritage churches, and the story of Dr. Jose Rizal. It is the old soul of Manila.

Food: Halo-halo is the ultimate Filipino summer cooler, a colorful mix of shaved ice, beans, nata de coco, ube, leche flan, sago, and ice cream.

LUZON

Banaue, Rice Terraces

Tinawon Red Rice

These ancient terraces are a UNESCO-listed wonder, shaped by generations of Ifugao farmers into giant green stairways that seem to reach the sky.

Food: Tinawon red rice is an heirloom grain with a nutty, earthy taste that feels deeply connected to the mountain soil of Banaue.

VISAYAS

VISAYAS

Boracay, Aklan

Chori Burger

Home to powdery white sand, crystal-clear waters, and unforgettable sunsets, Boracay is a beach destination known for energy, color, and island fun.

Food: Chori burger is a Boracay street-food favorite made with smoky native longganisa in a toasted bun, paired with spicy vinegar and atchara.

VISAYAS

Bohol

Peanut Kisses

Bohol offers the famous Chocolate Hills, the tiny tarsier, and relaxing river cruises, making it a place where nature and local charm blend beautifully.

Food: Peanut kisses are Bohol’s beloved pasalubong, a small meringue cookie with roasted peanuts, crunchy outside and sweetly nutty inside.

MINDANAO

MINDANAO

Siargao

Kinilaw

Siargao is the surfing capital of the Philippines, with coconut-lined roads, laid-back island culture, and breathtaking spots like Sugba Lagoon.

Food: Kinilaw is a fresh seafood dish cured in coconut vinegar, calamansi, ginger, onion, and siling labuyo, bringing out a bright sour-and-spicy flavor.

MINDANAO

Davao City / Samal

Durian

Set against the backdrop of Mt. Apo and Samal’s white-sand beaches, this region is rich in natural beauty, adventure, and the culture of Mindanao.

Food: Durian is the king of fruits, celebrated for its strong aroma and creamy, custard-like flesh that is both bold and unforgettable.
